This policy explains what personal information AGMIEX collects when you use agmiex.com or contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and specific to how this website actually works.

1. Who we are

AGMIEX (“AGMIEX”, “we”, “us”) is an IT services studio based at Devri Rd, Phase 2, Defence Estate, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282001, India. For the purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, AGMIEX is the data fiduciary for the personal data described here.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number, the service you’re interested in, an optional budget range and your project details.
  • Website demo form: your full name, business name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, an optional budget range and your requirements.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you choose to subscribe.
  • Direct messages: anything you share with us by WhatsApp, email, phone or Instagram.

Payments

Payments for the ₹99 website demo are processed by Razorpay on its own secure checkout page. We do not receive or store your full card, UPI or bank account details. Razorpay may share with us the details you enter at checkout (such as your name, email and phone number) together with the payment status.

Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The website stores your light/dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage so it’s remembered on your next visit; this never leaves your device. The servers that host the website may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotes, website demos and proposals, and to deliver the services you engage us for.
  • To confirm payments and bookings.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure, including filtering spam submissions.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We process personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form or contact us, and for other legitimate uses permitted by law. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and our business, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — stores form submissions securely.
  • Razorpay — processes payments.
  • Google — email (Gmail) and the embedded map.
  • Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram) — when you choose to message us there.

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside India. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Third-party content

Our contact section embeds a Google Map. When it loads, Google may receive your IP address and set its own cookies, as described in Google’s privacy policy. Our fonts are hosted on our own website, so no font requests go to third parties. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Razorpay are governed by those services’ own policies.

6. Retention and security

We keep enquiry and project records for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver our services and meet legal and accounting requirements. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored with access controls, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • confirm what personal data we hold about you and provide a summary of it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we no longer need it for a lawful purpose;
  • withdraw your consent to further processing.

Email agamsbusiness@gmail.com with your request. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you may raise a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

Our website and services are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Agam Singh, Founder, at agamsbusiness@gmail.com or +91 85348 55501.

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
